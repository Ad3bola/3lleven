Common questions

Do I need to provide content before you start?

Not at the design stage. We can start with rough ideas or placeholders and fill in real content during the build phase. If you have copy, logos, and images ready that's ideal — but it's not a blocker.

What if I need more revisions than included?

Additional revisions beyond the two included rounds are billed at an hourly rate, agreed upon before the project starts. Most projects don't need this.

Can I see a staging version before it goes live?

Yes — every project gets a staging link during the build phase. You review the real site, running live, before anything goes to your domain.

What happens after launch if I'm not on a monthly plan?

You get the source code, full credentials, and a walkthrough. You own everything and can manage it yourself or hire anyone to maintain it. There's no dependency on 3lleven to keep your site running.

How does payment work?

50% deposit to kick off the project, 50% on final delivery before the site goes live. Monthly plans are billed at the start of each month.
