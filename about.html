About 3lleven

Built different, on purpose.

3lleven is a one-person web studio based in Detroit, MI. No agencies, no hand-offs — just focused, custom work delivered directly.

The Story

Why 3lleven exists.

Most small businesses either overpay for agencies that treat them like an afterthought, or they settle for a DIY builder that looks generic and loads slowly. There was no obvious middle ground — something custom, fast, and actually affordable.

3lleven is that middle ground. Clean, custom websites built by one person who cares about the work. No bloated process, no mystery pricing, no account managers in the way. Just a good site, delivered fast, owned by you.

The name? 3lleven is a stylized take on "eleven" — a number that's always one step beyond what you'd expect. That's the goal on every project.

How We Work

The principles behind every project.

01

Flat fees, not hourly

You know the price before any work starts. No billable hours, no scope creep surprises. The number in the proposal is the number on the invoice.

02

You own everything

Source code, credentials, the domain — all of it transfers to you on final payment. There's no dependency on 3lleven to keep your site alive.

03

No templates, ever

Every site is designed and built from scratch. What we deliver is built specifically for your business — not a Wix theme with your logo swapped in.

04

Speed is a feature

Every site ships fast by default. Clean code, optimized assets, no bloated plugins. Performance isn't an add-on — it's how we build.
