Questions about cost.

The ones that come up before every project. If yours isn’t here, ask — a straight answer costs nothing.

What exactly do I pay, and when?

Two options, chosen at signing. Pay in full and it is 50% before work begins and 50% before the site goes live — nothing after that. Or pay across twelve monthly instalments: $210 for Basic, $340 for Standard, $550 for Enterprise. Paying monthly costs slightly more overall, and those totals are written into the agreement so there is no surprise.

If I pay monthly, am I locked in?

For the first six instalments, yes. After that you can end the agreement, but the unpaid balance of the build becomes due — the instalments are a way to spread a fixed price, not a subscription you can walk away from halfway through. If that is not what you want, pay in full instead.

Do I have to take the Care Plan?

No. It comes free with every build for the first 12 months and is required with none. Without it the site transfers to your own hosting at launch and you handle hosting, domain renewals and security from there, with 30 days of bug fixes from us. With it, we handle all of that at no cost for a year.

What happens after the first 12 months?

The Care Plan continues at $99/month unless you cancel it. We’ll write to you at least 30 days before the first charge so it’s never a surprise — if you’d rather stop there, just say so and nothing is billed.

Can I cancel the Care Plan?

Any time, with 30 days’ notice — during the free year or after. There is no minimum term. When you cancel we hand the site and its domain configuration over to you, or to whatever hosting you choose, at no charge.

Do I actually own the site?

Yes — the full source code, on every tier, under both payment methods. It is delivered by GitHub or ZIP once the final payment or final instalment clears. There is no fee for code ownership and no Care Plan requirement attached to it. You can move it anywhere.

What if I need more pages than the tier covers?

There is no per-page billing. Page count is one of the things that decides which tier fits — a brochure site is Basic, a multi-page site is Standard, a larger or multi-location build is Enterprise. If you are between two, we will tell you which one you actually need rather than selling you the larger one.

What is not included?

Two rounds of revisions come with every tier; further rounds are billed at $75 each. Work outside the agreed scope is handled as a change order, quoted before anything starts. Third-party costs you would pay anyway — domain registration, paid fonts, stock photography, any platform fees — are yours.

How long does it take?

Roughly two weeks from kick-off to launch for a new build, about ten days for a redesign, counted from the deposit landing and your materials arriving. Larger Enterprise builds take longer and get a real timeline in the proposal, not a guess.
