Project investment & payment terms

For projects over $1,000: 50% deposit / 25% at design approval / 25% at launch.

Payment method: All payments are processed via Stripe (credit/debit card or ACH). Invoices are sent via email. The deposit invoice is sent upon signing. Work begins only after the deposit is received and cleared.

Late payment: Invoices unpaid after 7 days from the due date are subject to a 5% late fee added to the outstanding balance. Project work will be paused until payment is received. The site will not be transferred or launched until all balances are cleared.

Monthly plan billing begins on the date the website goes live. Stripe automatically charges the card on file each month on that date. All monthly plans carry a 6-month minimum term (see Section 7). If a payment fails, Stripe will retry automatically. If payment remains outstanding after 14 days, the plan will be suspended until resolved.

Section 03

Revisions & feedback

Every project includes 2 rounds of revisions at no additional cost. A revision round is defined as a single consolidated list of changes provided by the Client in writing after reviewing a design or development deliverable.

  1. Each revision round must be submitted as one complete list — not multiple separate requests sent over time.
  2. Additional revision rounds beyond the two included are billed at $75 per round, invoiced before work resumes.
  3. Scope changes (adding new features, pages, or sections not in this agreement) are not revisions and are handled as Change Orders (see Section 9).
  4. Feedback turnaround: Client agrees to provide revision feedback within 5 business days of receiving a deliverable. Delays beyond this extend the project timeline accordingly.
Section 04

Client responsibilities

The Client agrees to provide the following materials in a timely manner. Delays in providing materials will result in equivalent delays to the project timeline:

  • All written content (text for each page) unless a copywriting add-on was purchased
  • Logo files in SVG or high-resolution PNG format (transparent background preferred)
  • Photos, videos, and brand assets to be used on the site
  • Access to any existing domain registrar, hosting, or CMS accounts as needed
  • Timely feedback and approvals — response within 5 business days to avoid delays
  • A single point of contact for all project decisions to avoid conflicting instructions

If the Client is unable to provide content within 14 days of a formal content request, 3lleven reserves the right to use placeholder content to maintain the project timeline. The Client remains responsible for updating placeholder content after launch.

Section 05

Project timeline

The estimated project timeline begins on the date the deposit is received. Standard timelines are as follows:

  • 1–3 page new build: 10–14 business days after deposit and all materials received
  • 4–6 page site: 14–21 business days
  • 7+ pages / e-commerce / complex builds: 21–35 business days (or as agreed in writing)
  • Rush delivery (shorter than standard timeline): subject to availability and a rush fee of 20% of the total project price

The timeline is contingent on timely receipt of all Client materials and feedback. 3lleven is not liable for delays caused by the Client's failure to deliver materials or approvals on time.

Section 06

Intellectual property & ownership

Upon receipt of full payment of all outstanding invoices, the Client owns the final website design and all custom assets created specifically for this project.

  1. Source code: Full source code is included with every plan — both one-time builds and monthly subscriptions. Code is delivered via GitHub or ZIP upon final payment (or upon request for monthly clients). There is no additional fee for code ownership.
  2. Third-party assets: Open-source libraries, licensed fonts, stock photos, and plugins used in the build remain subject to their original licenses. 3lleven is not responsible for future licensing changes to third-party tools.
  3. Portfolio rights: 3lleven reserves the right to display the completed website in its portfolio and to use it as a case study in marketing materials, unless the Client requests otherwise in writing.
  4. Prior to full payment: No ownership is transferred until all invoices are paid in full. 3lleven retains all rights to work-in-progress until that point.
Section 07

Hosting, maintenance & ongoing support

The website will be hosted on Cloudflare Pages (infrastructure provided by 3lleven under a monthly plan, or transferred to the Client's own hosting for one-time build clients).

Monthly plan clients: The selected plan (Basic, Standard, or Enterprise) covers hosting, security updates, backups, and support as detailed in the plan description. All monthly plans carry a 6-month minimum term from the date the website goes live. After the initial 6-month term, the plan continues on a month-to-month basis and may be cancelled with 30 days' written notice.

One-time build clients (no monthly plan): Hosting, maintenance, security updates, and domain renewals become the sole responsibility of the Client after the site goes live. 3lleven provides 30 days of complimentary bug fixes after launch for issues directly related to the build (not third-party changes or Client modifications).

Uptime: 3lleven uses automated uptime monitoring and will address outages within 4 business hours for monthly plan clients. 3lleven is not liable for downtime caused by platform-level incidents (Cloudflare, DNS providers, etc.).

Section 08

Warranties & limitation of liability

3lleven warrants that all work is performed professionally and that the website will function as described in this agreement at the time of delivery.

3lleven does not guarantee:

  • Specific search engine rankings or SEO outcomes
  • Sales, leads, revenue, or business results from the website
  • Compatibility with browsers or devices released after the project completion date
  • Uninterrupted uptime of third-party hosting platforms

In no event shall 3lleven's total liability to the Client for any claim exceed the total amount paid by the Client under this agreement. 3lleven is not liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages of any kind.

The Client is solely responsible for ensuring all content provided to 3lleven (text, images, logos, etc.) does not infringe on the intellectual property rights of any third party.

Section 09

Change orders & scope additions

Any work requested beyond the scope defined in Section 1 of this agreement — including additional pages, new features, integrations, or design changes that alter the agreed-upon direction — constitutes a Change Order.

  1. Change Orders must be agreed upon in writing (email is acceptable) before additional work begins.
  2. Each Change Order will include a price and estimated timeline for the additional work.
  3. Change Orders are invoiced separately and do not alter the payment terms of the original project.
  4. 3lleven reserves the right to decline Change Orders that would significantly alter the project scope or timeline.
Section 10

Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep confidential any proprietary information shared during the course of the project. This includes, but is not limited to, business strategies, pricing, login credentials, customer data, and any documents marked as confidential.

Credentials (hosting logins, domain registrar access, etc.) shared with 3lleven will be stored securely using encrypted credential management tools and deleted upon project completion or client request.

Section 11

Termination

Either party may terminate this agreement with 7 days' written notice (subject to the 6-month monthly plan minimum where applicable — see Section 7).

  1. Client-initiated termination: The deposit is non-refundable. If work has begun, the Client owes a prorated payment for all work completed up to the termination date, not to exceed the total contract price.
  2. 3lleven-initiated termination: If 3lleven terminates the agreement (e.g. due to Client non-communication exceeding 21 days, or a fundamental breakdown in the working relationship), the Client will be refunded any amount paid beyond the value of work completed.
  3. Non-communication: If the Client is unresponsive for more than 21 consecutive days after a written follow-up, 3lleven reserves the right to close the project, retain the deposit, and release any reserved time slots.
Section 12

Governing law & dispute resolution

This agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Michigan. Any disputes arising under this agreement that cannot be resolved informally between the parties shall be subject to binding arbitration in Wayne County, Michigan, in accordance with applicable rules.

Both parties agree to attempt good-faith resolution of any dispute through direct communication before initiating any formal legal process.

Section 13

Entire agreement

This agreement, together with any written project proposals, change orders, or addenda expressly incorporated herein, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions, negotiations, and agreements. No modification of this agreement is valid unless made in writing and signed by both parties.

By signing below, both parties confirm they have read, understood, and agreed to all terms set forth in this agreement.
